## Full-length practice tests
- Start with an official diagnostic test to identify weaknesses.
- Mid-prep: take an official full-length test every 10–14 days.
- Final stretch (last 6 weeks): increase to one official full-length test per week, simulating test day conditions (Bluebook app or timed PDF).
- Always do a deep review of every missed question and categorize mistakes by topic and reasoning error.

## Core tactics for a 1600 target
- Maintain an error log and tag mistakes by topic/subskill; re-solve each missed question until you can get it correct unaided.
- Prioritize deliberate practice — focus on weaker subskills with targeted problem sets rather than untargeted volume.
- Time your practice and simulate test conditions regularly; build stamina with frequent full-length practice in the final month.
