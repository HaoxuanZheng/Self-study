# AP Precalculus — Official Study Sources (College Board)

## Official College Board Resources

1. AP Course and Exam Description (CED) 2025–2026
   - Page: https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-precalculus
   - Notes: Download the CED PDF from the course page — it contains the course framework, unit learning objectives, sample questions, exam structure, and information about the formula/reference sheet.

2. Personal Progress Checks (PPCs) — AP Classroom
   - Portal: https://myap.collegeboard.org
   - Units:
     - Unit 1: Polynomial and Rational Functions
     - Unit 2: Exponential and Logarithmic Functions
     - Unit 3: Trigonometric and Polar Functions
     - Unit 4: Functions Involving Parameters, Vectors, and Matrices
   - Notes: PPCs are available in AP Classroom when assigned by your teacher; they’re ideal for unit-level practice and feedback.

3. Free-Response Questions (FRQs) & Scoring Guidelines
   - Page: https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-precalculus/exam/past-exam-questions
   - Notes: Download FRQs and scoring guidelines for past administrations. Practice timed FRQs and compare responses against the official rubrics.

4. Official Practice Exam (full-length)
   - Where to find: Exam resources on the AP Precalculus course page (CED / Exam Resources) or AP Classroom.
   - Notes: Use official full-length practice exams under timed conditions; review scoring guidelines for detailed feedback.

5. Formula Reference Sheet (allowed on exam)
   - Where to find: The CED / Exam Resources on AP Central include the official list of formulas/reference information provided to students. Confirm exact contents in the CED.
   - Notes: Memorize what is NOT on the sheet; practice problems that require memorized identities and formulas.
